Methodology

How CryptoVol constructs volatility indices, surfaces and Greeks from live options market data.

1. Data Collection

Option chain snapshots are collected from Deribit — the dominant venue for BTC, ETH and SOL options — three times daily, capturing the open of each major trading session:

  • Asia session — 02:00 UTC
  • London session — 08:00 UTC
  • US session — 16:05 UTC

For each snapshot, we collect the full option chain: bid/ask prices, strikes, maturities, and underlying spot and futures prices across all listed expiries.

2. Forward Curve & Yield Curve

We bootstrap a forward curve from listed futures prices and a yield curve from put-call parity across strikes and expiries. This gives us a consistent discount and carry framework for each snapshot.

F(T) = S × e^(r-q)T
Put-Call Parity: C - P = (F - K) × e^(-rT)

3. Vol Surface Calibration

For each expiry, we calibrate a SABR model (Hagan et al. 2002) to the observed market implied volatilities. The SABR model captures the volatility smile with four parameters:

SABR Parameters

  • α (alpha) — Level of implied vol (ATM vol proxy)
  • β (beta) — Set to 1 (lognormal backbone)
  • ρ (rho) — Correlation between spot and vol; controls skew
  • ν (nu) — Vol-of-vol; controls smile curvature

SABR Lognormal Formula (β=1)

σ_B(K,F) = A × (z/χ(z)) × B

where:
A = α / [(FK)^((1-β)/2) × (1 + (1-β)²/24 × ln²(F/K) + (1-β)⁴/1920 × ln⁴(F/K))]
B = 1 + [(1-β)²α²/(24(FK)^(1-β)) + ρβνα/(4(FK)^((1-β)/2)) + (2-3ρ²)ν²/24] × T
z = ν/α × (FK)^((1-β)/2) × ln(F/K)
χ(z) = ln[(√(1-2ρz+z²) + z - ρ) / (1-ρ)]
When F ≈ K (ATM), we use the limiting form: σ_ATM = α/F^(1-β) × B to avoid numerical instability. When z ≈ 0, χ(z) is computed via Taylor expansion: 1 - ρz/2 + (2-3ρ²)z²/24.

4. CryptoVIX Index

Our CryptoVIX is computed following the VIX methodology (model-free variance swap rate), adapted for crypto options:

Variance Swap Rate

VarSwap(T) = (2/T) × Σᵢ [ΔKᵢ/Kᵢ²] × e^(rT) × Q(Kᵢ)

where Q(Kᵢ) is the mid-price of the out-of-the-money option at strike Kᵢ. We integrate over all available strikes from 10% to 300% moneyness.

Constant Maturity

To produce a constant-maturity index (e.g. 7D, 30D), we compute the variance swap rate for each listed expiry then interpolate in variance space:

CryptoVIX(T*) = √[ interp(VarSwap, T₁, T₂, T*) ] × 100

5. Vol Smile Display

The vol smile chart shows SABR-interpolated implied volatility across the full strike range for each listed expiry. The SABR curve is evaluated at 200 equally-spaced strike points between the minimum and maximum quoted strike.

Market quotes (bid/ask midpoints) are shown as dots overlaid on the fitted curve, allowing users to assess calibration quality.

6. Term Structure

The ATM term structure plots the SABR ATM implied vol (closest quoted strike to the forward price) against each listed expiry date, showing how vol varies with tenor.
